# ArkheOS router

Route only. Do not perform another Skill's workflow here.

- Browse products or compare eligibility: `$catalog`.
- Sign in, activate the no-card trial, inspect membership, choose monthly or annual checkout, open the billing portal, or sign out: `$account`.
- Install an entitled signed ArkheOS product: `$install`.
- Check or apply a signed product update or exact rollback: `$update`.
- Inspect receipts, verify, export, recover, remove, or explicitly purge local state: `$recover`.

Customer product effects and reusable native bridges retain their own authority. Installation is not proof of activation or native application effect.
